People in Photographs privately delivers your photos and records, where the photographer asks, whether you let them publish them. Downloads never require permission.

Your link is private

Anyone holding your card link can access its gallery and submit a decision. Keep it safe. Possession of a link is not identity verification.

What is stored

Photos and, when you make a decision: the decision; when giving permission, a typed name, an optional email address, your credit preference and the name you want credited; an optional note; the image versions decided on; the wording shown; the time; the IP address, browser user-agent, accepted languages and browser-provided platform/client hints. The file you download is the photographer's original and keeps its camera details as they are, including where it was taken; the smaller versions shown on the page carry none of them. There are no advertising or analytics trackers. Essential form-security and language-preference cookies are used.

Your choices

Return to your card to change or withdraw permission. To have your photos deleted, contact the photographer{% if contact %} at {{ contact }}{% endif %}. When they delete them from the app, your gallery closes and the files are erased from the app's storage for good; copies the photographer keeps elsewhere, such as backups, are theirs to remove.

Retention

Data stays on the photographer's own server or storage until they remove it. Decision records, including names and IP addresses, are retained when photos are deleted; identity redaction is not yet implemented.
